XAI // Explainable Artificial Intelligence

Grad-CAM Activation & Failure Analysis

Deep neural networks often act as black boxes. Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) illuminates the exact pixel clusters that triggered the classification decision.

1

Gradient Tracking

During the forward-backward pass, gradients of the deepfake score are computed with respect to the spatial feature maps of the convolutional & attention layers.

2

Spatial Pooling

Gradients are globally pooled across channels to generate importance weights, capturing which spatial regions contain synthesis boundaries, warping, or blending seam artifacts.

3

Heatmap Overlay

The weighted 2D activations pass through a ReLU rectifier, are scaled to the input size, and mapped onto a JET colormap (Red = high attention, Blue = baseline skin).
